# Professions of the Silo

> **Numbering note (2026-09-06).** This file predates the insertion of Article 10

> the name is authoritative. Current numbering: 10 Mines, 11 Supply, 12 Commerce,

> 19 Health, 20 Assembly, 21 Records, 22 Emergency, 23 Amendment.

A brainstorming reference, not yet-drafted content: a long, categorized
list of trades and offices across the silo, to support Article
13 ("Of Labor, Shadowing, and the Assignment") and to
keep every other Article's incidental mentions of trades consistent with
one another. Organized by Department, matching the Pact's own structure
(`FACTS.md` tracks what's actually been drafted; this file is inventory
for what hasn't been yet — nothing here is canon until it appears in
actual Article text).

## How to read this list

Each profession is tagged along three axes:

- **Shadow** — the entry path, per the shadowing system already
  established across Articles 6, 8, 9, and 10 (Article 13 defines
  the mechanism itself):
  - **Full** — a long apprenticeship (years) under a departing
    tradesperson; for skilled, technical, or high-trust work.
  - **Short** — an abbreviated shadowing period (weeks/months); for
    moderately skilled but lower-stakes work.
  - **None** — simple task assignment, little or no formal training;
    the default for unskilled or entry-level labor.
  - **N/A — sentenced, not shadowed** — reserved for the two roles that
    aren't vocations at all: mine labor (Article 17's punitive tier) and
    cleaning (Article 18). Included here only to mark clearly that they
    sit *outside* this whole system, not as items on equal footing with
    the rest.
- **Training/licensing** — whether the role touches Article 8's
  dangerous-instrument licensing regime, carries Order-adjacent secrecy
  beyond ordinary citizenship (per Article 1/8's "gesture without
  specifics" device), or needs no special status beyond its shadow.
- **Zone** — Up Top, Mids, or Down Deep, per Article 1's zoning
  (a trade can span zones; this marks its primary floor).

## Cross-cutting quick reference

**Full-shadow trades** (the backbone of skilled labor): Head of every
Department, Generator Tender, Electrician, Machinist, Structural
Inspector, Welder, Server Technician, Wire/Radio Technician, Sensor
Technician, Cleaning-Lab Technician, Relic Examiner, Lens-Grinder,
Hydroponics Grower, Seed-Keeper, Livestock Tender, Butcher, the Judge,
Officer of Judicial, Relic Classifier, Sheriff, Deputy Sheriff,
Physician, Midwife, Teacher, Chit-Keeper, Tailor, Cobbler.

**Short-shadow trades**: Reclamation Worker, Freight Lift Operator,
Instrument Licensor, Stores Clerk, Cook, Sanctioning Clerk, Judicial
Archivist, Census Clerk, Floor Peacekeeper, Clerk of the Mayor's
Office, Infirmary Aide, Mind-Tender, Nursery Attendant, Market Clerk,
Housing Clerk, Head Porter.

**No/informal-shadow trades**: Mechanical Runner, Distribution Runner,
Porter.

**Article 8-licensed trades** (touch the dangerous-instrument regime):
Electrician, Machinist, Welder, Wire/Radio Technician, Lens-Grinder,
Freight Lift Operator (logging only, not the same category but adjacent).

**Order-adjacent / high-secrecy trades** (per the "gesture without
specifics" device — never explained in-text, just gestured at): Server
Technician, Sensor & Wallscreen Technician, Cleaning-Lab Technician,
Relic Examiner, Seed-Keeper, Physician (via the Structural Fragility
crack), Teacher (via the unstated deep-lore connection).
