\chapter{Of the Sheriff and the Keeping of the Peace}
\label{art:sheriff}

\articlenote{In which is set down the charge of the Sheriff: the ordinary peace of the floors, the good order of the stair and every landing upon it, and the keeping of common ground common.}

\section{The Charge of the Sheriff}
\label{sec:sheriff:charge-of-the-sheriff}

The Sheriff is charged with the ordinary peace of the floors, distinct from the Officers of Judicial under \artref{art:judicial}, and holds office Up Top as \artref{art:founding} sets down.

\begin{clauses}
\item The Sheriff is appointed by the Mayor, who shall weigh the recommendation of the Sheriff then leaving office but is not bound by it, and may appoint to the office any citizen of majority, whether a Deputy of the Sheriff's office or not.
\item A Deputy or Peacekeeper of the Sheriff's office serves under the Sheriff in the ordinary conduct of that trade, and remains bound in every other respect to this Pact and to Judicial, exactly as any other citizen is bound.
\item The office of Sheriff is barred to a citizen afflicted with the Syndrome, and vacated by it, as \secref{art:labor}{sec:labor:trades-of-trust} provides; upon such a resignation the Mayor appoints a new Sheriff as this section provides.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Stair and the Landings}
\label{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings}

The stairwell, which \artref{art:founding} holds open to every citizen equally, is the Sheriff's particular charge to keep orderly, for it is the one road every citizen must walk, and disorder upon it endangers more than the citizens who make it.

\begin{clauses}
\item A citizen ascending the stair keeps to the outer rail, and a citizen descending keeps to the inner rail, that the press of citizens moving in opposite directions not itself become a hazard; the Sheriff may set this practice aside, by posted notice, where a floor's own landing requires it.
\item A porter under \artref{art:records}, a citizen carrying an urgent word for Judicial or the Sheriff, and a work crew under posted notice under \artref{art:founding}, pass before any citizen not so charged, and no citizen may impede that passage.
\item No citizen may block, occupy, or make camp upon the stair or a landing beyond the ordinary pause for rest, nor raise a hand against another citizen upon it; an offense under this clause, given the height from which a citizen upon the stair may fall, is weighed by the Judge with that danger in mind.
\item The Sheriff shall keep a Deputy or Peacekeeper posted at such landings as the press of citizens requires, particularly at the landings nearest the cafeterias, the markets, and the floors of Supply and Mechanical.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Cafeterias and Other Common Areas}
\label{sec:sheriff:cafeterias-and-other-common-areas}

The cafeterias, the markets, and every hall where citizens gather beyond their own household are likewise the Sheriff's charge, that gathering not become disorder.

\begin{clauses}
\item The Sheriff shall keep such order in a cafeteria, a market under \artref{art:commerce}, or a common hall under \artref{art:assembly} as the press of citizens requires, and may disperse a gathering that threatens to become disorder, though the Sheriff may not disperse an assembly properly held under \artref{art:assembly} for the assembly's purpose alone.
\item Where a cleaning under \artref{art:cleaning} draws citizens to a cafeteria's wallscreen, the Sheriff shall keep the peace of that gathering, and no citizen may be compelled to attend nor barred from attending, save where the press of citizens itself endangers the floor.
\item The freight lift under \artref{art:mechanical}, the offices of every Department, and any floor or room this Pact reserves to a particular charge are not common areas under this section, and a citizen found there without leave is answerable under \artref{art:crimes} as though the citizen had trespassed upon a restricted area.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Keeping of the Peace Between Citizens}
\label{sec:sheriff:keeping-of-the-peace-between-citizens}

The Sheriff shall answer a dispute or a wrong between citizens under \artref{art:crimes}, and shall carry to Judicial any matter the Sheriff cannot resolve by word alone, or any matter \artref{art:crimes} names among the offenses answered by the mines or among the gravest.

\begin{clauses}
\item A citizen may bring a complaint to the Sheriff directly, and the Sheriff shall hear it and act upon it, or refer it to Judicial, within a turn of the season of its bringing.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Trades of the Sheriff's Office}
\label{sec:sheriff:trades-of-the-sheriffs-office}

Every trade of the Sheriff's office below the Sheriff's own is entered by the shadowing set down under \artref{art:labor}; the office of Sheriff itself is not shadowed, and is filled as \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:charge-of-the-sheriff} provides.

\begin{clauses}
\item The Sheriff then leaving office shall pass to the Mayor, and to whoever succeeds as Sheriff, a full accounting of the floors and landings under Deputy or Peacekeeper charge, and every posted notice given under this Article.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Station and Its Cells}
\label{sec:sheriff:station-and-its-cells}

The Sheriff keeps a station in the Up Top, beside the cafeteria and its wallscreen, and this station is the one place in the silo, apart from the airlock and the Infirmary's isolation under \artref{art:health}, where a citizen may be held against the citizen's will by any office but Judicial.

\begin{clauses}
\item The station holds the Sheriff's own office, the desks of the Deputies, the roll of posted notices under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:posted-notices}, and no fewer than two cells, each with a bed, water, and a light that may be dimmed but not put out; no cell is below ground beyond the station's own floor, and no citizen is held in any room of the silo not a cell of this section or of a Deputy's station under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:deputies-and-the-floors}, save by Judicial in the hall of Judicial itself.
\item One of the cells of the station bears a screen upon its wall, kept by Information Technology under \artref{art:it} and not by the Sheriff, which shows what the outward sensors show, as the wallscreen of the cafeteria shows it. The screen is lit at every hour. It is not darkened, it is not covered, and it is not turned from the citizen held; the Sheriff has no hand upon it. No citizen is put in that cell save a citizen held for the departure under \secref{art:cleaning}{sec:cleaning:days-between-the-request-and-the-departure}, and such a citizen is put in no other.
\item The station is not a common area under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:cafeterias-and-other-common-areas}; a citizen enters it to bring a complaint, to be held, to visit a citizen held, or at the Sheriff's asking, and for no other purpose.
\item The Sheriff keeps a station roll in which every citizen held is entered with the hour of holding, the cause, the Deputy who held them, and the hour of release or of delivery to Judicial; the roll is delivered to the Clerk of Judicial at each turn of the season and entered in the archive.
\item A citizen held in a cell is fed the ration, may be visited by the citizen's household once in each day of holding, and may send word to Judicial by the Sheriff's own hand, which the Sheriff shall carry the same day.
\end{clauses}

\section{Arrest and the Holding of a Citizen}
\label{sec:sheriff:arrest-and-the-holding-of-a-citizen}

The Sheriff holds a citizen only to carry the citizen to Judicial, or to keep the peace until Judicial can be reached, and not as any measure of correction, which is Judicial's alone under \artref{art:crimes}.

\begin{clauses}
\item The Sheriff, a Deputy, or a Peacekeeper may take hold of a citizen found in the act of an offense, or named in a warrant of the Judge under \artref{art:judicial}, or whose remaining at liberty the Sheriff finds, upon a complaint brought, to endanger another citizen; or a citizen held for the departure under \secref{art:cleaning}{sec:cleaning:days-between-the-request-and-the-departure}; and for no other cause.
\item A citizen taken hold of is told the cause at the taking, and is brought to the nearest station under this Article or \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:deputies-and-the-floors}, and entered in its roll, before any other thing is done.
\item A citizen held for an offense among the gravest under \artref{art:crimes} is carried to Judicial the same day, by the Sheriff or a Deputy with an Officer of Judicial sent for the purpose. A citizen held for any other cause is carried to Judicial, or released, within one cycle; a citizen not carried within the cycle is released by the Sheriff without further order, and the release entered, whatever the cause of the holding; save a citizen held under \artref{art:cleaning}, who is held until the departure that Article provides.
\item A citizen released under this section is not thereby cleared; the complaint stands in the Sheriff's hands, and may be referred to Judicial under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:keeping-of-the-peace-between-citizens} as any other, and the citizen taken hold of again upon a warrant.
\item The Sheriff may not question a citizen held as to an offense beyond the citizen's name, floor, and household, nor take from the citizen anything but what endangers the cell; what is taken is entered in the station roll and delivered with the citizen to Judicial, or returned at release.
\end{clauses}

\section{The Deputies and the Floors}
\label{sec:sheriff:deputies-and-the-floors}

The stair is long, and the Sheriff cannot be at every landing; the Deputies are the Sheriff's presence where the Sheriff is not.

\begin{clauses}
\item The Sheriff keeps three Deputies, each with the charge of a zone as \artref{art:founding} names them: a Deputy of the Up Top, who keeps the station under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:station-and-its-cells} in the Sheriff's absence; a Deputy of the Mids, who keeps a station of the Sheriff on a floor of the Mids the Sheriff names; and a Deputy of the Down Deep, who keeps a station on a floor of the Down Deep the Sheriff names, near the works and the workings. Each Deputy's station has a cell, a roll, and the copy of this Pact \artref{art:founding} requires.
\item The three stations are the standing establishment of this Article. They are kept whether or not the floors upon which they stand ask for them, and no zone is at any hour without a Deputy within it. The Sheriff assigns to each zone not fewer than two Peacekeepers besides the Deputy, and a zone left with fewer for longer than one cycle is reported by the Sheriff to the Mayor with the cause.
\item Where a Deputy's post falls vacant, the Sheriff names a Peacekeeper of that zone to keep it until the Sheriff names a Deputy; a Peacekeeper so keeping has a Deputy's whole authority within that zone and none beyond it, and may not be named Deputy on that account alone.
\item Each Deputy keeps such Peacekeepers as the Sheriff assigns to the zone, posts them at the landings \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings} requires, and reports to the Sheriff at each turn of the season the floors kept, the complaints brought, and the citizens held.
\item A Deputy acts with the Sheriff's whole authority within the zone, save that a Deputy may not appoint a Peacekeeper, set aside the practice of the stair under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings}, or refer a matter to Judicial in the Sheriff's name without the Sheriff's word; word between the Sheriff and a Deputy passes by porter under \artref{art:records} or, where the silo's peace cannot wait, by the stations of \artref{art:it} as official word.
\item Where the Sheriff's office falls vacant, the Deputy of the Up Top keeps the station and the whole charge of this Article until the Mayor appoints a Sheriff under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:charge-of-the-sheriff}, and may not be appointed Sheriff on that account alone.
\end{clauses}

\section{Posted Notices}
\label{sec:sheriff:posted-notices}

What the Sheriff requires of the floors, the floors are told in writing, at the landing, in the Sheriff's own hand or a Deputy's.

\begin{clauses}
\item A notice of the Sheriff is posted at the landing of every floor it binds, states the matter, the floors it binds, the day it takes effect, and the day it ceases or that it stands until taken down, and bears the Sheriff's or the Deputy's name; a notice not so posted binds no citizen.
\item A notice may set aside the practice of the stair under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings}, close a landing to the passage of a work crew under \artref{art:founding}, name the hours a common area is kept, call the citizens of a floor to the Sheriff's hearing of a complaint, or give any other direction this Article allows the Sheriff; it may not forbid a citizen the stair, nor a common area, beyond the hours and the crew's passage those Sections allow.
\item Every notice posted is entered in the roll of notices at the station with the day of posting and the day of taking down, and the roll is passed to the succeeding Sheriff under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:trades-of-the-sheriffs-office}; a citizen may read the roll at the station upon asking.
\item A citizen who takes down, defaces, or covers a posted notice before its day is answerable under \artref{art:crimes}; a citizen who believes a notice exceeds this Article may petition Judicial under \artref{art:judicial}, and the notice stands until Judicial rules.
\end{clauses}

\section{What the Stair Carries, and When It Is Closed}
\label{sec:sheriff:carrying-and-closing-of-the-stair}

The stair is the one road, and what cannot be carried upon it cannot be carried at all; the Sheriff keeps it passable, and closes any part of it only as this section allows.

\begin{clauses}
\item A citizen carrying a burden too wide for two citizens to pass beside keeps the inner rail, whether ascending or descending, and cries the burden at each landing passed. Such a citizen yields to those named in \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings} and to no other, and every other citizen yields to such a citizen.
\item A load which two citizens cannot carry between them does not go upon the stair at all, but by the lift, and is entered in its log under \secref{art:records}{sec:records:freight-lift-log}; a crew that takes such a load upon the stair is stopped where it stands by the Deputy of that zone and is answerable to the Sheriff.
\item A citizen dead is carried down and not up, by the inner rail, a Deputy or a Peacekeeper going before, and every citizen upon the stair stands to the rail and lets it pass. No citizen is required to stop, to stand, or to say anything, and no citizen is hindered who does. Where the giving to the soil under \secref{art:health}{sec:health:giving-to-the-soil-and-the-mourning} is upon a floor above, the lift carries the body and the stair is not used.
\item The Sheriff may close a run of the stair between two named landings, and not more than one run at a time, for not more than one day without the Mayor's word, by notice under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:posted-notices} set at both landings and at the landing above and the landing below. The whole stair is never closed, by the Sheriff or by any other office, save under \artref{art:emergency}.
\item Where a run is closed, the Sheriff names the hours in which a citizen who must pass is passed, and no closure keeps a citizen from the Infirmary under \artref{art:health}, from a sitting of the Assembly the citizen is called to, or from a departure under \artref{art:cleaning}; a citizen so passed goes with a Peacekeeper and is entered in the station roll.
\item At the turning of the shifts the stair is at its fullest. The Sheriff posts those hours at every landing, and no work crew takes a load upon the stair within them save upon a matter the Sheriff enters as urgent and names in the entry.
\item Where the press at a landing market is such that the stair cannot be kept, the Deputy of that zone may set the market's hours or close it for the day, by notice at the landing, and enters the closing with its cause. What a trader could not sell upon a day so closed is not counted against that trader's measure under \artref{art:commerce}.
\item A citizen may rest upon a landing and may not be moved from it for resting, the ordinary pause for rest under \secrefhere{sec:sheriff:stair-and-the-landings} being no occupying of the stair; and a citizen who cannot keep the stair without resting is not thereby kept from it.
\end{clauses}
