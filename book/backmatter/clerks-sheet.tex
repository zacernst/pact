{\scshape Amendment 21.} The Measure Beyond the Third Term.

{\scshape It alters:} \secref{art:crimes}{sec:crimes:reckoning-of-a-term-in-the-mines}, by addition.

{\scshape Day of its ratifying:} the sixth day of the third cycle of the second season.

{\scshape Its words, entire:}

\begin{sloppypar}
``\secref{art:crimes}{sec:crimes:reckoning-of-a-term-in-the-mines} is amended to add: `Where a citizen who has served three terms commits a further offense answered by the mines, the Judge shall not sentence the citizen to a term, and shall instead assign the citizen to labor in the workings under \artref{art:mines} --- not as a term, and not as a sentence, but as the assignment of the citizen's labor under \artref{art:labor}, the silo's need of hands in the workings being continual; and the citizen so assigned is housed in the Down Deep under \artref{art:housing}, draws the ration and the chits of a citizen in labor, keeps the household's dwelling, and is in every respect a citizen in a trade and not a citizen under sentence. The assignment is entered by the Clerk, is reviewed by the Head of Mines and the Judge together at every turn of the season, and continues while the silo has need of the labor.' The remainder of \secrefhere{sec:crimes:reckoning-of-a-term-in-the-mines} is unchanged.''
\end{sloppypar}

{\scshape Of its bringing.} It was brought after Amendment 20, upon the
Mayor's proposal at the Judge's asking, the Judge having reported to the
Assembly that a citizen then before Judicial had served three terms, had
committed an offense not among the gravest, and could not be sentenced at all;
and having reported that every measure this Pact allowed was forbidden to the
case, asked the Assembly for one.

\vspace{2pt}
{\scshape Note of the Clerk.} This copy was printed before the amendment was
ratified and could not carry it. Until this copy is printed afresh, this sheet
is the amendment, and binds as the amendment binds. The reader is directed to
strike \artref{art:crimes} from the list of unamended provisions at the end of
the Log that follows, where it stands in error in this copy and in every copy
printed before this day.
