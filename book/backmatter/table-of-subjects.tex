\section*{A Table of the Subjects of This Pact, with the Articles and the Sections Where They Are Found, Kept by the Archivist of Judicial}

\noindent Kept by the archivist of Judicial, as \secref{art:records}{sec:records:copying-and-keeping-of-this-pact} requires of every printed copy, that a citizen may come to the law by the thing the citizen came to ask about. A subject is entered against the Article and the Section where it is treated, and not against the leaf on which it falls, so that one table serves a copy however the copy is set. A reference is read as the Article and then the Section: \textsc{18.4} is the fourth Section of the eighteenth Article, and a numeral standing alone is a whole Article.

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent What is a subject, and under what word it is entered, the archivist judges. The table is no part of this Pact: it binds nothing, it is cited in no hearing, and a subject wanting from it is not thereby wanting from this Pact.
